Why ReLU?

ReLU (Rectified Linear Unit) is the most widely used activation in modern deep learning. Unlike tanh and sigmoid it doesn't squash values into a fixed range — it simply passes positive inputs through and clamps negative ones to zero.

ReLU(z) = max(0, z)
ReLU′(z) = 1 if z > 0, else 0
